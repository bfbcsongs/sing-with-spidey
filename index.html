🎤 Karaoke Editor V1
1. Select your MP3/WAV/OGG file. 2. Paste the lyrics. 3. Tap "Prepare Lyrics". 4. Tap "Start Timing". 5. Play the song. 6. Press SPACE or tap the "Next Word" area while each word is being sung. 7. The timing of every word is recorded. 8. Stop Timing when finished. 9. Use the word timing list to manually correct timing. 10. Save Project or Export JSON. Keyboard shortcuts: SPACE = timestamp next word while timing P = Play/Pause ← = Back 5 seconds → = Forward 5 seconds The project is stored locally in your browser.
